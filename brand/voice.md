# Kettlebird Tea: Brand Voice Guide

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. We are launching The Steep Club, a monthly tea subscription box for people on our waitlist.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Warm. Comfortable. Relieving.**

We sound like a kind friend who puts the kettle on at the end of a long, tiring day. Our emails should feel like a deep breath and a chance to relax.

## 2. Words we use

- relaxing
- cosy
- warm

Other words that fit the same feel: unwind, settle in, gentle, a quiet moment.

## 3. Words we avoid

- quick
- hurry up
- too late

Avoid anything rushed or pushy. We are a brand about slowing down. That also rules out "Act now", "Don't miss out" and shouting in capital letters.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- Never promise any medical benefit. No "cures stress", "helps you sleep" or "good for your health".
- Never say a doctor or other expert backs, approves or recommends our tea.
- Never call our tea "the best tea".

## 5. How we sign off

Every email comes from the team, not one person.

> Warmly, from us to you,
> The Kettlebird Team
