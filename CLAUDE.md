# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- **brand/** – Brand voice, logo notes, colours and style guidance.
- **leads/** – Lists and notes about the people and groups we want to reach.
- **campaigns/** – Campaign plans and the email drafts themselves.
- **notes/** – Ideas, research and anything else worth keeping.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. Keep sentences short and avoid jargon.
- Never send email to real people. Only write drafts and plans.
- The one exception is sending through Resend. That is allowed only to Resend's test addresses (ending in @resend.dev) and to my own email address. A guard will enforce this in the next step.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
