# The Steep Club Launch: Campaign Plan

**Brand:** Kettlebird Tea
**Dates:** Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)
**Channel:** Email only. No paid ads.
**Status:** Draft plan. No emails have been sent.

---

## 1. Campaign Overview

**Name:** Put the Kettle On

**Summary:** We invite our waitlist into The Steep Club, a monthly tea box, with warm, gentle emails that fit each person's reason for joining.

**Primary goal:** 300 waitlist members start a subscription within 4 weeks of launch.

That is 15% of about 2,000 people. It is a bold target for a waitlist. Waitlist members asked to hear from us, so it is possible. But we should treat 300 as the goal and watch the numbers weekly (see section 7).

**Secondary goals:**
- Get at least 60% of the waitlist to open at least one email.
- Learn which of the three groups is most ready to subscribe.
- Collect a few kind words from new members for later campaigns.

---

| **Daily tea drinkers** | Already drink tea every day | New favourites, good variety, and a nicer daily ritual. | Decision |

**Group sizes:** Not yet known. Please add the numbers once the list is split. It will help us set per-group targets.

**What they share:** They are busy and a bit tired. A calm moment is the thing they are really buying.

## 3. Key Messages

**Core message:** A cosy box of tea, every month, for your quiet moment.

**Supporting messages:**

1. **Slow down.** The Steep Club is a small, relaxing pause built into your month.
   *Proof:* Describe what is in the box and how it arrives.
2. **No tea know-how needed.** Each box comes with simple steeping notes.
   *Proof:* A photo of a real card from a box. *(Needs a sample.)*
3. **A gift that keeps arriving.** Warm thoughts, once a month.
   *Proof:* Gift option details. *(Decision needed, see section 10.)*
4. **Fresh favourites for people who already love tea.** New blends to settle into.
   *Proof:* A list of the first month's teas.

**Tone by group:**
- Beginners: gentle and reassuring.
- Gift buyers: thoughtful and unhurried.
- Daily drinkers: warm and a little more detailed about the teas.

**Voice rules to follow in every email** (from the brand guide):
- Use words like relaxing, cosy, warm, unwind, settle in.
- Avoid rushed or pushy words. No "quick", "hurry up", "too late", "Act now" or "Don't miss out". No capital-letter shouting.
- Make no medical claims. No "helps you sleep" or "good for your health".
- Never say a doctor or expert backs our tea. Never call it "the best tea".
- Sign off every email: *Warmly, from us to you, The Kettlebird Team.*

---

## 4. Channel Strategy

**Email only.** It suits a waitlist, because these people already gave us their address and asked to hear from us.

- **Why it fits:** It is free to send, easy to tailor by group, and easy to measure.
- **Effort:** Medium. About 12 emails to write, plus one landing page.
- **Formats:** Short, warm emails with one clear button each. Plain layout, one or two photos.
- **Supporting pieces (no extra cost):** The Steep Club page on our own website, and a line in our order confirmation emails for existing customers.

**How we send:**
- One shared email for everyone at launch and at the final reminder.
- Group-specific emails in between.
- Anyone who subscribes stops getting sales emails and moves to a welcome email.
- Anyone who clicks but does not subscribe gets one extra gentle follow-up.

## 7. Success Metrics

**Primary KPI:** 300 new Steep Club subscriptions from the waitlist by 3 January.

**Pace check (to stay on track for 300):**

| End of | Target subscriptions (running total) |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | 120 |
| Week 2 | 200 |
| Week 3 | 250 |
| Week 4 | 300 |

Most sign-ups usually come in the first days after a launch email. These weekly marks are a starting guess. We should adjust them after week 1.

**Secondary KPIs:**
- Open rate: aim for 50% or more across the waitlist.
- Click rate on the sign-up button: aim for 8% or more per email.
- Landing page to sign-up rate: the share of visitors who subscribe.
- Unsubscribe rate: keep under 1% per email. If higher, we are sending too much.
- Sign-ups by group: shows which message works best.

**How we track:** Email tool reports, plus the sign-up count from the shop. Use a different link on each email so we know which one led to each sign-up.

**Reporting:** A short check every Monday. A full summary after the campaign ends.

## 9. Risks and What We Will Do

1. **300 is a high bar (15% of the list).**
   *What we will do:* Check the numbers at the end of week 1. If we are behind, use the open calendar slots for a different angle, and tell the team early rather than late.

2. **The holiday season is busy and inboxes are full.**
   *What we will do:* Keep emails short. Pause sending over 24 to 26 December. Keep the tone calm.

3. **Gift orders may need to arrive by a certain date.**
   *What we will do:* Confirm the first box dates and delivery times before writing email 4. Only promise what we can deliver.

4. **Something goes wrong with sign-up on launch day.**
   *What we will do:* Test the full sign-up the week before. Have a named person watching on 7 December.

5. **Emails sound too salesy.**
   *What we will do:* Check every draft against the brand voice guide before it goes out.
